## My Favorite Series is Alice in Borderland

<strong>Alice in Borderland</strong> is a Japanese sci-fi thriller series based on the manga <em><strong>I’m a Hero in Borderland</strong></em> by Haro Aso. The story follows Arisu Ryohei, a disillusioned young man who along with his friends, is transported to a deserted and dangerous alternate version of Tokyo. In this world, they must participate in life-or-death games, each categorized by different suits (hearts, diamonds, clubs, and spades), testing their intelligence, physical skills, and survival instincts. As Arisu and his companions fight to survive, they uncover the dark secrets of the Borderland and its mysterious creators, all while grappling with the psychological toll of the brutal games.

### My Reason 
- The series is filled with constant twists and high-stakes challenges, making it impossible to guess what will happen next. Each game pushes the characters to their limits, keeping the suspense and excitement alive throughout.

- The characters are not one-dimensional; they grow emotionally as they face life-and-death situations. Arisu’s journey from a disillusioned young man to a determined survivor is deeply engaging.

- The show brilliantly combines physical survival challenges with mind games that test intelligence and morality. This unique blend keeps the narrative fresh, as every episode forces both the characters and viewers to think critically.
